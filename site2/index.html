01/ about

About

Profile, field of interest and working languages.

I'm Bibek Subedi, a Civil Engineering graduate of IOE Pulchowk Campus (B.E. 2026) and a prospective graduate student. I work on flood and debris-flow reconstruction, hydrological modelling with machine-learning diagnostics, design rainfall, and earthquake hazard in the Kathmandu Valley. HEC-RAS, SWAT, mHM, OpenSees, Google Earth Engine and Python are tools I've learned through research rather than coursework.

Four of my papers (three as first author) are accepted for GeoMandu 2027, and two journal manuscripts have been submitted. I am a research cohort member at Kathmandu Geo Lab, and I lead an eco-friendly sanitation project in Dhading under WorldLink's CSR programme.

Away from the drawing board I teach. Over 2,000 students a year sit the IOE entrance exam with my guidance, and a community of more than 3,000 follows the engineering and mentorship videos I make on YouTube. I sketch, I trek, and I photograph the mountains I walk through.

series R

Research Studies

RES-01 Aug 2026 — present

Rasuwa Rock-Ice Avalanche and Debris Flood

Two-dimensional reconstruction of the 26 August 2026 debris flood over 195 km from Rasuwagadhi to Devghat, checked against a satellite-mapped footprint (critical success index about 0.72) and six DHM gauge timings, with exposure screening of buildings, roads and hydropower.

HEC-RAS 6.6 2D · Sentinel-1 SAR (Earth Engine) · DEM differencing · r.avaflow · Python

RES-02 Lead author · submitted

Residual Learning as a Model Diagnostic, Arun Basin

Gradient-boosting correction of a calibrated multi-site SWAT model raised Nash–Sutcliffe efficiency from 0.53–0.66 to 0.70–0.81 at three gauges and showed that small pooled biases hid 21–33% overestimation of the lowest flows; mHM/mRM calibrated to KGE 0.847 (validation 0.836).

SWAT-CUP · gradient boosting · LSTM baseline · SHAP · mHM 5.13 / mRM

Code (Zenodo) ↗

RES-03 Lead author · submitted

Design Rainfall for Urban Storm Drains, 52 Indian Cities

Located a 1975 gauge-network jump in IMD gridded rainfall and separated it from trend; Monte Carlo tests showed the drainage manual's decadal-update rule raises 5-year design depths in half of trend-free records, while a one-sided likelihood-ratio test kept false alarms near 7%.

Break tests · GEV / L-moments · nonstationary GEV · Monte Carlo · Python

RES-04 4 papers accepted

Earthquake Hazard in the Kathmandu Valley

Borehole-driven Newmark atlas of earthquake-induced slope displacement, Saltelli–Sobol sensitivity of nonlinear site amplification (6,144 OpenSees runs) and GIS-AHP / maximal-coverage siting of early-warning sensors, accepted for GeoMandu 2027.

OpenSees · Newmark · Sobol · GIS-AHP · maximal coverage
